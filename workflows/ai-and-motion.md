# AI features and motion

Use the source-backed [MindLeverX rationale and AI proposals](../projects/mindleverx/mindleverx-design-rationale.md) as a worked example. Its HAX, W3C and Carbon sources support the design considerations; the proposed features are not implemented or validated by that document.

## AI interaction checks

- State what the feature can and cannot do. Label illustrations and synthetic data.
- Connect claims to supporting source excerpts, collection dates and limitations.
- Distinguish missing collection from an observed absence, and uncertainty from a numerical confidence claim.
- Test unsupported questions, stale/conflicting evidence, incorrect citations, correction, cancellation, retry and failure states.
- Evaluate latency, cost and useful task completion separately from visual polish.

## Motion checks

- Give each transition a purpose: orient, connect cause/effect, reveal a relationship or acknowledge an action.
- Specify trigger, duration/easing, start/end states and interaction interruptions.
- Provide reduced-motion behavior that preserves the information and action.
- Check animated contrast, focus stability, pause controls where applicable and performance on real devices.
