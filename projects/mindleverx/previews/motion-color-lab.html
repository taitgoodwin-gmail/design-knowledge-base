Deep blue and a precise evidence diagram give the work an analytical, atmospheric feel. The service statement stays prominent; moving lines remain a secondary explanation.

Hypothesis: this presentation may make the service easier to understand. No user study or conversion test has established that outcome.

Principles, accessibility and implementation
  • Visual hierarchy gives the offer priority over the brand and decorative effects.
  • Proximity, connecting lines and numbered labels group the answer with its evidence.
  • Color reinforces meaning; it is never the only distinction. Text remains visible throughout.
  • Replay runs once for 1.1 seconds. That duration and easing are design choices, not an industry standard.
  • Stop immediately restores the complete static connection. The operating-system preference and Reduce motion control both disable the animation.
  • This prototype does not query an AI service or present real measurements.

Source basis: WCAG use of color, pause, stop, hide, and animation from interactions (AAA). Full rationale and Figma limitations are in the repository's motion/color review.
