# Motion, color and material decisions

Checked 20 September 2026. Thirteen additional official Figma articles were reviewed in the selected scopes in the [review record](2026-09-20-motion-color-review.json). These are documentation findings, not thirteen new hands-on passes. Existing [E09 evidence](lab/motion-and-vector.md) remains a bounded manual reveal and export test.

## Choose motion by its job

| Element or operation | Documented capability or constraint | Why we would use it for MindLeverX | Color and movement interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citation connection | Path trim requires a centered stroke. The preset applies to an open vector path; manual trim keyframes support open or closed paths. | Trace a visible answer excerpt to its source, making the relationship legible. This is a proposed interaction, not an accuracy claim. | Keep both endpoints and their text visible. A moving accent can reinforce the relation; labels and a static connecting line carry the same meaning without motion. |
| Entry and state change | Presets start at the playhead; duration and composite tracks can be adjusted. Manual motion uses property keyframes; auto-key records edits when enabled. | A short reveal can orient attention to a user-requested detail. Leave the service explanation and primary action immediately readable. | Animate a detail's opacity/position only after checking every relevant background state. Avoid transient low-contrast text. |
| Easing | Figma offers linear, eased, back, spring and custom curves. Its entry/exit suggestions are recommendations, not universal usability laws. | Use a restrained ease-out for a requested disclosure; reserve spring behavior for small playful feedback that does not delay a task. | Motion and saturation both attract attention. Avoid making every colored object move. Timing values remain design choices to test. |
| Rotation and scale | Motion positions use the anchor point; Design positions use the upper-left corner. Anchor position also governs rotation and scaling. | Place the origin deliberately before animating a citation marker or small status cue. | An incorrect origin creates apparent drift that can make a mark seem detached from its label. |
| Curved travel | Motion paths expose keyframe positions and curve handles; intermediate dots express pacing. | Use only when a curved path helps explain a connection. Straight transitions are easier to follow for ordinary disclosures. | Paths should remain distinct from decorative atmospheric lines. Preserve a static labeled endpoint. |
| Reusable sequence | Animated instances can move in time; their duration and animated properties are changed on the main component. Component properties are edited in Design. | Reuse a checked evidence-card sequence rather than independently varying each card. | Pair reusable animation with semantic color tokens, while testing each actual surface and state. |
| Export | Animation export selects a top-level frame. MP4, WebM, GIF and SVG are documented; higher resolution/frame-rate options are plan-gated. | Choose the implementation format by fidelity, control and delivery needs; inspect the actual exported artifact. | A rendered video or GIF does not automatically inherit website motion preferences, control semantics or alternate text. |

Sources: [S60: Motion](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41274629073303-Explore-Figma-Motion), [S61: Presets](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41307886266135-Quickly-add-motion-with-preset-animation-styles), [S62: Keyframes](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41307938657559-Add-select-and-delete-keyframes), [S63: Easing](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41414048690839-Adjust-an-animation-s-easing), [S64: Anchors](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41352588622615-Move-a-layer-s-anchor-point), [S65: Paths](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41780233501591-Edit-an-object-s-motion-path), [S66: Trim](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/42200302811927-Animate-strokes-with-path-trim), [S67: Animated components](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41307940738967-Create-and-use-animated-components), [S68: Export](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/41307983648407-Export-animations-from-Figma).

The easing article's description of Hold needs a dedicated test before adopting a particular step-timing interpretation. No new native presets, trim, anchor, animated-component or export-format experiments were performed in this review.

## Make atmosphere portable and readable

[S69: Gradients](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/34208860210199-Use-gradients-as-a-fill-or-stroke) documents linear, radial, angular and diamond types. Stops can use variables; complete gradients can be shared as styles. The embedded legacy video is not the current interface authority. Our choice: use an atmospheric gradient behind a stable text surface, then inspect the actual blended colors. Endpoint token contrast alone does not establish contrast across the gradient.

[S70: Effects](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041488473-Apply-effects-to-layers) documents glass, shadows, blur, noise and texture with order-dependent rendering. Glass is not supported in SVG export or Sites; an opaque fill hides its appearance. Glass and background blur cannot both render on the same object as independent effects. Layer blur and unclipped texture may extend beyond bounds and affect blending; grouped layers can render differently. Our choice: keep glass optional and provide a simple translucent/solid surface that preserves readability in the intended destination. Noise should add texture without carrying data or reducing legibility. These selected effects sections were reviewed; not every shadow setting was examined.

[S71: Color models](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043042113-About-color-models) distinguishes color notation from the file's rendering profile. Changing Hex/RGB/HSL notation does not itself change the rendered color. [S72: Profiles](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039825114-Manage-color-profiles-in-design-files) distinguishes Assign, which retains values while changing interpretation, from Convert, which changes values to approximate appearance. Conversion can lose out-of-gamut color and does not convert color styles or variables. Branch merging does not transfer the file profile; version history does not distinguish the conversion operation. Our choice: record the profile and operation, inspect token values and export settings, and verify the deployed result. No file profile was changed here.

## Recognized accessibility requirements and optional enhancements

The following W3C Understanding pages were rechecked on 20 September 2026. They explain the normative criteria; they are not substitutes for a whole-site conformance evaluation.

| Basis | Required scope or distinction | Our implementation choice |
|---|---|---|
| [WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.1, Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html), Level A | Color must not be the only visual means of conveying the relevant information. | Label evidence, errors and selected options; add meaningful shapes or text where appropriate. |
| [SC 1.4.3, Contrast Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), Level AA | Generally 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for qualifying large text, with specified exceptions. | Measure actual foreground/background combinations, including animation states. |
| [SC 2.2.2, Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html), Level A | Automatically starting moving content lasting over five seconds alongside other content needs a control unless essential. Auto-updating information has separate conditions and no five-second exemption. | Keep this design comparison static until requested; provide Stop and a complete static endpoint. |
| [SC 2.3.3, Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html), Level AAA | Nonessential interaction-triggered motion can be disabled. This is not an AA criterion. | Respect the operating-system reduced-motion preference and offer an explicit reduced-motion control as an additional design choice. |

These principles support a restrained, distinctive direction. They do not prove that a particular palette, animation duration, atmospheric effect or AI feature will improve conversion. Validate those hypotheses with representative users and measured task outcomes.
