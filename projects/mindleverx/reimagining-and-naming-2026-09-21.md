## Naming assessment and domain clarification

The assistant described MindLeverX as technical and energetic, with an X-based graphic opportunity; Levarum as more abstract, compact, and open to sophisticated or playful treatments. These are creative judgments, not measured audience findings. Both names require a clear explanation of the service. No validated linguistic origin, customer-preference result, or naming-clearance conclusion was established.

## Recognized design foundations and evidence status

The sources below were reviewed or retrieved during the 2026-09-20 discussion. Applying a framework does not prove that a particular palette, wordmark, animation, or concept improves conversion.

| Foundation | Proposed application | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Visual hierarchy and Gestalt grouping | Prioritize the customer problem, offer, evidence, and next action; keep findings connected to supporting sources. | [NN/G visual design principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/) |
| Progressive disclosure | Explain the service immediately and make deeper methodology available on request. | [NN/G progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) |
| Consistency, feedback, recognition, and user control | Predictable navigation, understandable states, and reversible interactions. | [Nielsen's usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) |
| Accessibility | Design toward WCAG 2.2 AA; verify relevant contrast, keyboard, focus, and reflow criteria. Reduced-motion support is also a chosen design practice, without claiming every motion preference is an AA requirement. | [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) |
| Purposeful motion | Use restrained task feedback and occasional expressive emphasis. | [IBM Carbon motion](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/motion/overview/) |
| Human-AI interaction guidance | Communicate capability and limitations, support correction, and tie explanations to evidence. | [Microsoft human-AI guidelines](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ai-guidelines/) |
