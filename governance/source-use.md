# Source and asset use

This repository is private. Original authored research and user-provided references are preserved for the requested knowledge collection. It does not grant a blanket license to republish third-party publications, artwork, screenshots, fonts or video.

Use original summaries with descriptive links; retain attribution and known limitations. The visual-study reference manifest records screenshot provenance uncertainty. Review an asset's actual license/permission before using it in a public product. Do not infer authorship from a repost account or a visual resemblance.

Notion imports are historical records; prices, benchmarks, instructions and platform claims were not independently revalidated during copying. Linked external Drive documents and media are references, not downloaded contents unless separately present in the local manifest.
