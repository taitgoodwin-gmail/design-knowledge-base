# TikTok design discovery

Research began 7 September 2026; updated 8 September 2026. This adds a TikTok discovery chapter to the existing research library. It does not replace the material/style study or select a Levarum direction. No Chrome operations, messages, follows, purchases or site edits were performed by this researcher.

## What was actually accessible

Web searches returned repeated robots restrictions for TikTok profile/video searches. However, two indexed TikTok channel pages returned readable post captions and actual video links. I opened five linked video URLs; every individual landing page returned zero text. **No videos were watched; no video transcripts or audio were obtained.** The descriptions below are discovery leads based on channel-page captions, not completed reviews of the videos. Channel content differed between search snippets and opened pages, so opened-page link/caption associations were used rather than assuming their search results stayed identical.

Opened discovery pages:

- [Graphic Design Styles](https://www.tiktok.com/channel/graphic-design-styles?lang=en): supplied captions and links for four items below. Page also contained a server-rendering error marker, but readable entries remained.

## Source-account lead: Denny Kurien

The [RGD member profile for Denny Kurien](https://rgd.ca/members/profile/dkurien), opened and read, explicitly distinguishes **Instagram @dkurien** from **TikTok @denny.kurien**. This is a useful correction to searching the visible screenshot handle directly on TikTok. The same profile identifies Kurien as Rayvn's co-founder and creative director. It supports account identity; it does not prove the exact ten-style montage was his original creation or that he made the designs featured within it.

The profile's website link led to [Rayvn's studio site](https://www.rayvn.io/), which was opened and read. Its published process separates discovery, direction/moodboards, then logo and broader identity work. That makes it useful for studying the creator's method beyond short-form aesthetic lists. This is the studio's own stated process, not independent proof of project outcomes.

## How to use this chapter

Use these links as a viewing queue, and extract concrete decisions after playback: the brief, what changed, why, how the typography and images cooperate, and where the author distinguishes taste from performance. Record whether an example is commissioned work, a speculative concept, a tutorial or a critique. A reel's reach or an engaging reveal is not evidence that its visual choices improve a service website. The combined discovery result is five real links from indexed channels, the exact matching ten-style post identified by the root operator, four additional root-inspected profile leads, and a verified cross-platform identity. Full video inspection remains separate work.
