## Lessons to carry into Augmind

1. **Attach the actual current frame.** The first Make preserved inspected palette, typography, evidence-card layout and prism asset. This was useful observed fidelity, not pixel-perfect proof.
2. **Specify a complete visitor flow.** Claim selection plus a usable draft download revealed defects that a polished homepage could not expose.
3. **Separate exact copy from implementation notes.** “Not a giant numbered poster” leaked into visible method copy. Review all generated copy.
4. **Verify artifacts and transitions.** Opening the downloaded file proved more than clicking its button. Back navigation exposed lost draft state; skip navigation exposed a hash-router collision.
5. **Recheck claims after visual refinements.** The later homepage introduced unsupported performance figures despite the original bounded fictional brief. A visual score does not verify truthfulness.
6. **Test actual responsive widths.** Initial320px evidence layout fit, while Reset width still missed the project 44px preference. These are distinct results.
7. **Maintain one writer and durable checkpoints.** Queue focused fixes; export actual code; preserve originals before local continuation. Credit or browser interruptions must not erase progress or produce false completion claims.

These are reusable methods. They do not show conversion lift, improved AI visibility, or representative-user comprehension. Augmind should get its own task/content model after this slice is validated; do not copy the evidence explorer indiscriminately.
