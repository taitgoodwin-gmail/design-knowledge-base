# Source-grounded design review

Use the [playbook](../principles/uiux-design-playbook.md), [worked cases](../learning/design-judgment-worked-cases.md), [element template](../templates/element-rationale.md), and [source policy](../governance/knowledge-policy.md).

Inspect the actual interface and state before interpreting it. Establish the task and audience. Record the observation, plausible alternative explanations, relevant requirement/guidance, proposed change, expected outcome and a proportionate check. A source can justify a design consideration without proving the chosen remedy improves conversion.

Compare concepts using the same substantive content. Examine navigation, hierarchy, typography, spacing, focus, target size, reflow, contrast, loading/error states, disclosure, and reduced-motion behavior where applicable. Record omissions; do not claim a full audit from a limited sample.

For each material choice, explain how color and movement support information or interaction. Test the result with real content and relevant users/devices before making user-outcome claims.
