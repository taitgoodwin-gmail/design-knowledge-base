# MindLeverX visual direction study

Date checked: 20 September 2026.

User direction: bold, polished, atmospheric, distinctive, with some playfulness. Exclude aggressive gothic styling. Explain GEO and the offer immediately; make evidence prominent. References are visual inputs, not verified performance or conversion models.

## Proposed directions — design choices

1. **Signal Observatory — recommended lead.** Deep blue, a luminous citation ring, large customer-focused typography, acid-lime accents, and warm-white evidence sections. Hero: “Your next customer is asking AI. Will it mention you?” The signature interaction connects an answer excerpt to its source. Intended to combine atmosphere with a legible explanation of the service.
2. **Human Current.** Warm charcoal, copper light, cream, expressive serif headlines, and restrained motion. Hero: “Be part of the answer.” Follow the atmospheric opening with a concrete annotated example. No entrance gate.
3. **Proof Studio.** Paper white, ink, citron, coral, oversized typography, a distinctive X motif, and editorial annotations. Hero: “Make your evidence stand out.” Evidence cards can become a dimensional gallery with an accessible list view.

All three are visual hypotheses for review. None has been validated for conversion or GEO outcomes. Keep fictional examples visibly labeled; do not imply that sample metrics are collected results. The current site's audit-availability limitation is retained in the concept preview.
