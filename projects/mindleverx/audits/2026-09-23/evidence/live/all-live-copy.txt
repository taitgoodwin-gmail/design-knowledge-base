L349: The working method
L350: Seven stages. Evidence at every step.
L351: An engagement starts with a clear question, preserves the evidence behind each finding, and returns to the same measurement after a change.
L352: Implementation status
L352: This is the intended service workflow. This website presents the planned method and sample reporting. Public intake is not open yet. Engine monitoring, automated audits and email delivery are not connected. Sample figures demonstrate a format; they are not results.
L353: 01
L353: Readiness request
L353: Begin with the company domain and the question the audit should answer. Separate a request for measurement from the completed measurement itself.
L353: Output:
L353: An intake record and a clearly stated scope.
L354: 02
L354: Agree what is measured
L354: Confirm the brand, category, competitors and questions before collecting evidence. Record which engines and collection conditions will be used.
L354: Output:
L354: An agreed scope and draft prompt panel.
L355: 03
L355: Lock the panel and preserve a baseline
L355: Keep a versioned copy of the prompts and record collection dates, sources and methodology. A later change to the panel starts a new comparison period.
L355: Output:
L355: A baseline with traceable evidence, when a real collection has been completed.
L356: 04
L356: Walk through the findings
L356: Review technical access, content quotability, off-site authority and measured visibility separately. Leave missing dimensions unmeasured; do not manufacture a composite.
L356: Output:
L356: Findings with sources, limitations and a proposed fix path.
L357: 05
L357: Choose what gets fixed
L357: Review the recommendations and decide which changes belong in scope. Record the decision and the reason before work proceeds.
L357: Output:
L357: An approved set of changes, or a documented decision to hold.
L358: 06
L358: Draft and verify the fixes
L358: Prepare content and technical artifacts for review. Check each draft against the approved scope and its underlying evidence. Publication is a separate action.
L358: Output:
L358: Reviewed drafts and verification notes.
L359: 07
L359: Measure again
L359: After an approved change is implemented, collect a comparable reading against the preserved panel. Treat delays, failed collections and missing evidence explicitly.
L359: Output:
L359: A new evidence record and a comparison only where the method and provenance match.
L360: Stages 01–04 establish the initial evidence. Stages 05–07 form a review and measurement loop. Re-baseline explicitly when the agreed scope or methodology changes.

L371: 01 · The practice
L372: Small, and specific about it.
L377: MindLeverX is a single-operator practice.
L377: There is no team page because there is no team yet. That is a deliberate disclosure rather than an omission: a practice that sells measurement should not begin by asking you to take an unmeasured claim about itself on trust.

L513: 05 · FAQ
L514: Frequently asked.
L517: Q1
L517: Is GEO just SEO with a new name?
L518: No. GEO shares SEO's technical foundations — crawlability, structure, quality — but optimizes for a different outcome: being cited inside a generated answer rather than ranking a link a human clicks. The overlap is real, which is why GEO layers on top of SEO instead of replacing it.
L521: Q2
L521: Which engines does GEO target?
L522: The generative answer engines your buyers use — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's Gemini / AI Overviews, and Claude — plus the AI features now embedded in classic search. Because each synthesizes answers differently, GEO monitors visibility across all of them rather than optimizing for one.
L525: Q3
L525: How do you measure GEO if there are no rankings?
L526: With answer-engine monitoring: citation rate (how often your pages are cited), mention rate (how often your brand is named), and share of voice (your citation share versus the category). The
L526: sample readiness scorecard
L526: illustrates a possible reporting format. Daily engine collection and a validated scoring rubric are not connected in this build.
L529: Q4
L529: How long until GEO shows results?
L530: There is no delivery or outcome guarantee in this preview. A meaningful comparison requires actual collection dates, a fixed prompt panel and enough evidence to separate a change from ordinary engine variation.
L533: Q5
L533: What does a GEO engagement actually involve?
L534: The intended workflow agrees scope, preserves a baseline, reviews findings, records approval and prepares changes before measuring again. The
L534: seven-stage method
L534: separates those steps and identifies what is not connected yet.
